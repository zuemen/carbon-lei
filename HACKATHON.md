## Submission requirements
- Track selection + problem alignment
- Project description (problem, solution, scalability)
- Demo video **3–5 min**
- Public code repository with documentation
- Working prototype / proof of concept

## Judging criteria
Climate Impact · Innovation & Creativity · Technical Execution · Practical Usefulness · Presentation & Communication

## Guardrails
- Separate project from `zuemen/agent-passport` (Monad Metropolis). vLEI know-how may be shared; **code is not copied between the two repos**.
- All companies in demos are **fictional**. No real LEIs, no real company names.
- Demo emissions figures are illustrative and are **not** claimed to follow the official CBAM calculation methodology.
- Keys and tokens only from environment variables; `.env` is git-ignored.
