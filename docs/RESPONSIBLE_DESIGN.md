# Responsible design: organiser themes and where we stand

The organisers' Session 3 on ethics, responsibility and good practices (9 October 2026) [1] asked participants to consider a list of themes. This page maps each one to what CarbonLEI does, with a link to the evidence, and to the gap or risk we know of. It adds no new claims; the linked sections carry the details. No outside party has reviewed this project, and no organisation has been contacted.

| Theme | What we did | Known gap or risk |
|---|---|---|
| Responsible AI | The product runs no AI model: all eight checks are deterministic code, and check 8 applies fixed rules and only warns ([README — AI usage disclosure](../README.md#ai-usage-disclosure); [FAQ](FAQ.md) Q8). AI use in development is disclosed ([HACKATHON](../HACKATHON.md#ai-usage-disclosure)). | Claude drafted much of the code and text and ran our adversarial reviews, including the contract red-team review ([SECURITY §4.2](SECURITY.md#42-findings-of-the-contract-red-team-review-7-october-2026)). These are AI-run reviews, not an outside audit. |
| Bias | No model, so no learned bias; the same proof gives the same verdict for every party. | The rules encode our reading of CBAM. They favour suppliers whose installation has a verified report from an accredited body; others fall back on default values, which CarbonLEI does not change. |
| Transparency | MIT licence; contract source verified on Etherscan; a check that cannot run is reported as skipped, never as passed ([ADOPTION §4](ADOPTION.md#4-how-easy-it-is-to-adopt)). Each document says what is measured, inferred or not done. | The GLEIF root is simulated and real vLEIs are untested ([FAQ](FAQ.md) Q9). |
| Accountability | Each report is signed by a listed verification body and anchored by a named lead auditor holding a vLEI role credential (ECR); revocations are synced on-chain, and reports registered within 24 h before a sync are CONTESTED for a person to review ([ARCHITECTURE §8](ARCHITECTURE.md#8-time-ordering); [FAQ](FAQ.md) Q27). | One allowlist owner key can list bodies, replace a body's address and transfer ownership (T10, CR1; [SECURITY §11](SECURITY.md#11-residual-risk-summary)). The fix (multisig, delays) is only drafted ([SECURITY §13.1](SECURITY.md#131-cr1-rotation-and-revocation-by-a-freshly-installed-address)). Reports a body falsified have no on-chain closing path (T3). |
| Data privacy and governance | Selective disclosure (the demo proof hides 5 fields; [README — How it works](../README.md#how-it-works)); identifiers on-chain only as salted commitments; no names or emissions values on-chain ([SECURITY §7](SECURITY.md#7-privacy-of-on-chain-data)). Personal-data reading against GDPR and EDPB Guidelines 02/2025 ([SECURITY §7.1](SECURITY.md#71-personal-data)). | Volumes and supplier addresses are visible and can be linked (T15). The lead auditor's AID hash is likely personal data on an immutable chain; erasure is not solved and no impact assessment has been done. We do not claim GDPR compliance. |
| Inclusivity | Importers verify free, with no wallet; suppliers need their LEI and a wallet, no vLEI, and pay only claim gas ([ADOPTION §3](ADOPTION.md#3-who-pays), [§4](ADOPTION.md#4-how-easy-it-is-to-adopt)). The demo has an automated accessibility test (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard-only; [`demo/e2e/a11y.spec.ts`](../demo/e2e/a11y.spec.ts), results in [README — Measurements](../README.md#measurements)) and 44 px tab and link targets at phone widths ([`demo/src/styles.css`](../demo/src/styles.css)). | Verification bodies pay for vLEI credentials. GLEIF says each issuer sets its fees [2]; one QVI publishes US$300 for a first Legal Entity vLEI (US$200 for a small business) and bundles from US$200 a year, with no ECR price listed [3]. Non-EU verifiers depend on accreditation capacity: 7 of 24 accreditation bodies accepted non-EU applicants on 7 October 2026 ([ADOPTION §2](ADOPTION.md#why-these-users-first)). The interface is English only, with no translation. |
| Stakeholder needs | Roles, keys and costs per party ([OPERATE](OPERATE.md#operating-model-at-a-glance)); pilot metrics without targets ([PILOT §4](PILOT.md#4-success-metrics)). | No user testing; only a scripted timing ([JUDGING_CRITERIA](JUDGING_CRITERIA.md#scripted-task-timing-not-user-testing)). Needs are inferred from public sources, not from interviews. |
| Security | Threat model, tests, fuzzing, mutation testing, bounded symbolic checks ([SECURITY — Summary](SECURITY.md#summary); [README — Measurements](../README.md#measurements)). | No external audit (T16). The vLEI checks use exported evidence and query no witnesses ([SECURITY §4.1](SECURITY.md#41-limits-of-the-vlei-checks-in-the-prototype)). |
| Environmental impact of the tool | Proof-of-stake chain; one transaction per report plus one per shipment; no token; reads send no transaction ([Footprint of CarbonLEI](CLIMATE_IMPACT.md#footprint-of-carbonlei)). | The footprint uses mainnet averages from 2022 as a proxy; Sepolia has no energy data. KERI agents and watchers are not estimated. |
| Measurable impact | We report the declared-emissions gap against the CBAM default, in tCO2e and €, not a reduction ([CLIMATE_IMPACT — Summary](CLIMATE_IMPACT.md#summary)). | Inside the CBAM Registry the added effect is about zero ([§4.4](CLIMATE_IMPACT.md#44-attribution-what-carbonlei-itself-adds)); we know of no observed rate of over-claiming, so we give no tCO2e avoided. |
| Scalability | Gas measured from 3 to 5,006 reports on a local chain ([ADOPTION §6.1](ADOPTION.md#61-technical)). | The tonnage cap binds only buyers that check the same deployment ([ADOPTION §5.1](ADOPTION.md#51-participation-when-the-cap-binds)); production chain not chosen. |
| Sustainability and long-term value | Contracts have no proxy or owner over records; if the operator stops, records stay readable and anyone can verify ([OPERATE — If the operator stops](OPERATE.md#if-the-operator-stops)). No token ([FAQ](FAQ.md) Q26). | Without the owner key nobody can list bodies or take over; an unplanned handover needs a redeployment. Handing the allowlist to a governance body is a proposal only. |
| Trust and trustworthy design | The verdict names each check and its status; [ADOPTION §5.1](ADOPTION.md#51-participation-when-the-cap-binds) lists what a pass guarantees and what it does not. | CarbonLEI does not prove that the measured emissions are correct or that goods come from the installation (T6, High). CBAM does not recognise vLEI or on-chain records ([ADOPTION §7](ADOPTION.md#7-adoption-risks)). |

**Worth fixing, in our view:** the English-only interface (suppliers in Taiwan are the first users) and the single owner key. Both are listed here, not done.
