CarbonLEI
Judge brief · CBAM · vLEI · Ethereum Sepolia

A carbon number is only as trustworthy as the person who signed it.

1Who signed?

The lead auditor's vLEI role credential (ECR), checked against a GLEIF-rooted chain (simulated root in the demo).

2Authorised at the registering block?

At the block that registers the report, the contract checks that the body is active and accredited and the auditor is not revoked.

3Tonnes claimed once?

Within this deployment: the ledger deducts each shipment from the 500 t verified and rejects a batch claimed twice or a claim beyond what remains.

Try to break it: four attacks
  1. 1Tampered value (1.8 edited to 1.2 tCO2e/t): check 2 fails, DISCLOSURE_TAMPERED; no contract is called.
  2. 2Tonnes claimed twice, or 400 t when 300 t are left: reverts BatchAlreadyClaimed / ExceedsVerifiedTonnage (dry run).
  3. 3Auditor who left (ECR revoked, watcher syncs): next report reverts AuditorNotAuthorized on Sepolia, status 0 · tx 0xa8b6b8a8…fc5
  4. 4Impostor body listed with a stolen owner key (simulated): contract accepts, status 1; check 7 rejects, wrong root · tx 0x295abe6c…013
Real
scale

EU imports of CN 7318 from Taiwan, 2025 (Eurostat): 371,270 t. Each 1% declared on a verified value instead of the 2026 default moves about 11,060 tCO2e of declaration onto verifiers' signatures.

Measured

  • 19/19 · 0/28 · 4/4 tampered detected · valid rejected · borderline flagged, on inputs we wrote
  • 524,065 gas: one report + one claim (Sepolia gas has no value)
